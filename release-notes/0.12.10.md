Cosign 0.12.10: far less relay traffic.

- Servers ping each other once a minute instead of every 10 seconds, and count a server as up for two and a
  half minutes after hearing from it. They no longer ping devices on a timer: a phone or browser that pings a
  server (every 10 s while it runs) is answered at once, so an open app still sees its servers up and a closed
  one costs nothing. Every member pinging every other every 10 s, in every account, was most of a shared
  relay's traffic.
- A decryption piece sent without its proofs is refused, and the partner that sent it is named (live finding
  16a). Every server since 0.12.7 sends them.
