Cosign 0.12.0 (beta)

- A home screen: search people, notes and hashtags, a Discover feed, and a bell. Your account, devices, sites and backups are behind your picture.
- "I already have an account": paste your nsec, and the phone splits it with your servers and forgets it. Make your backup right after.
- Security fixes from two reviews: a paper backup or a removed device gets nothing more from a server (no piece of a decryption); sites connected by code can't read or send messages (use the app or extension); only FROST (RFC 9591) signing; a new account needs two servers, and a paper is always worth 2. Servers connect only to the address they checked.
- Report lost is final. Taking your account out and removing your servers' pieces wait 3 days, and any of your devices can stop them.
