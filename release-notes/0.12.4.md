Cosign 0.12.4 (beta)

- Search finds people properly: the relays that answer fastest first, the people you follow before anyone else, and names ranked by an exact match, an address that checks out, and who follows them. Copies of one profile — bridges and bots — fold into a single row you can open. Paste a link to someone or a post and it goes straight there.
- Marble's key work runs in a process of its own, apart from the feed and everything it loads.
- Your server's page: choose who can join it (only you, the people you follow, the people you invite, or anyone), see the accounts it serves, and stop serving one.
- Joining a server you were invited to: your phone shows it whose account this is, signed, so a server can take on the people its owner knows without handing out codes. A code from its page still works, and is still the only way for a brand-new account.
- What a server sees, what searching sends, and where pictures come from: written down in the README.

Search and the folding were checked on an emulator against live relays, not yet on a phone.
