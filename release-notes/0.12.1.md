Cosign 0.12.1 (beta)

- Create your account in the app: your phone and two or three of Cosign's servers make it together, so no one, this phone included, ever holds all of it. Then your paper backup.
- Take your account out of Cosign (Advanced): with your paper backup at once, or after a 3-day wait any of your other devices can stop. You get your nsec back as you had it. You can also remove your servers' pieces (after the same wait).
- Discover: threads open at once, pictures open full screen (zoom, swipe), videos play in the card or full screen.
- Paste a code instead of scanning one (sign-in codes, nostrconnect://).
- Use your own LibreTranslate server (https, with an optional API key) to read notes in other languages.
- Servers let go of accounts a stranger started and never finished setting up. A finished account is never deleted for being idle.
